A movement in motion

The road to independence

India's freedom movement grew across decades, shaped by mass participation, political organising and many forms of resistance.

1857

The uprising of 1857

A widespread rebellion challenged East India Company rule across northern and central India.

1885

Indian National Congress founded

A new national forum gave political leaders a platform to organise and press for reform.

1930

The Salt March

Gandhi and his followers marched to Dandi, making salt in defiance of British colonial laws.

1942

Quit India

A mass campaign called for an immediate end to British rule, despite widespread arrests.

1947

Independence

India became independent on 15 August, after generations of struggle.

The legacy we carry

A freedom shaped by many hands.

The independence movement was never a single story or a single strategy. Millions took part: students, workers, farmers, writers and organisers, remembered and unremembered.

01

Collective courage — ordinary people found strength in acting together.

02

Many approaches — peaceful protest, political organising and revolutionary action all marked the struggle.

03

Voices still heard — their hopes for justice and self-determination remain part of the story.
